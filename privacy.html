Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Introduction

BuzzLead ("we", "our", or "us") operates a scheduling platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and profile details you provide when creating an account.
  • Calendar data: When you connect Google Calendar or another calendar provider, we access your availability solely to enable scheduling. We do not store calendar event contents.
  • Booking information: Names, email addresses, and additional fields collected when a meeting is booked.
  • Usage data: Basic logs used to maintain and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the scheduling Service.
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notices.
  • Sync with connected calendar providers (e.g., Google Calendar).
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Google Calendar Integration

Our Service uses the Google Calendar API to read availability and create calendar events when meetings are booked. Access is granted only through Google's secure OAuth 2.0 flow. We request the minimum scopes necessary and do not access, store, or share your calendar data beyond what is required to provide scheduling functionality.

You may revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted via Railway. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect against unauthorized access.

6. Data Sharing

  • Service providers: Third-party services necessary to operate the platform (e.g., Google APIs, email delivery), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of BuzzLead or others.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at operations@buzzlead.io.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and authentication state. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
